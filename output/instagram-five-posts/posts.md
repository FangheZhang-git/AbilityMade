## 1. Meet AbilityMade

Good work deserves to be seen. 💚

That’s why AbilityMade began: two students wanted to help more people discover the work of disabled artisans. Today, our website brings together handmade gifts, craft objects, and artwork while supporting people with intellectual, developmental, and physical disabilities.

Come have a look around at abilitymade.net. You might find something that feels very you.

A small detail can be the thing you love most.

A pattern. A color pairing. The way a piece feels in your hand. AbilityMade’s story is rooted in artisans spending time learning techniques and carefully making their work by hand. That care is part of what we hope you notice.
