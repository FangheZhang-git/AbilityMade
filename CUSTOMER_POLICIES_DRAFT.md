## 1. Shipping / 配送

**English — proposed customer copy**

Our approved shipping scope is all 50 US states, DC, Puerto Rico (PR) and Guam (GU); AS, MP, VI and UM are excluded.  Standard shipping is $10 per order when the merchandise subtotal is below $120, and free when it is $120 or more, before tax. We charge only one shipping fee per order, even when products come from different artists or suppliers.

AbilityMade collects customer payments through Stripe, coordinates orders and pays suppliers’ shipping fees. Independent artists and suppliers ship directly to you. Items in one order may arrive separately, with different tracking references. Contact AbilityMade for help with any part of your order.

**Proposed estimates, subject to owner and supplier approval:** for ready-to-ship items delivered within the contiguous US, allow 3–7 business days for preparation and dispatch, followed by approximately 3–7 business days in transit (approximately 6–14 business days total). Business days exclude weekends and US federal holidays. These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Any longer lead time for made-to-order or oversized items must appear on the product page before purchase. Alaska, Hawaii, military-address and other destination estimates must be confirmed before those destinations are offered; the Checkout country setting does not itself establish carrier coverage.

If shipment is delayed beyond the stated dispatch window, we will contact you with a revised shipment estimate and the option to accept the delay or cancel unshipped items for an appropriate refund. We will not silently extend the dispatch date. Contact us if a package is missing, damaged, or incorrect; AbilityMade will coordinate the resolution with the supplier. Tracking will be provided when available; multiple packages do not create additional customer shipping charges.

## 2. Returns, refunds and cancellations / 退货、退款及取消订单

**English — cancellation and damaged-arrival decisions approved; ordinary return terms and processing deadlines remain pending**

- Contact AbilityMade@gmail.com within 30 calendar days after delivery of each item to request a return. Ordinary returns must be unused, in their original condition, with original packaging where reasonably possible. After return approval, send the item within 14 calendar days. Contact us before sending anything: return locations differ by supplier. Do not send returns to an address on a parcel without our instructions.
- For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays return postage; the original $10 shipping fee is not refundable. No restocking fee or retroactive shipping charge is proposed, including when a partial return takes the retained merchandise below $120.
- **Approved damaged-arrival policy:** if a product arrives damaged, contact AbilityMade@gmail.com with your order reference and photographs showing the damage and, where relevant, the packaging. AbilityMade will evaluate the issue and provide an appropriate refund when justified, consistent with applicable consumer rights. Photos support review; inability to supply them does not automatically remove statutory rights. No automatic refund or replacement is promised. Handling of defective/incorrect goods, required returns and return-postage responsibility remains to be finalized, without limiting applicable rights.
- **Approved cancellation policy:** after purchase, we do not accept voluntary order cancellations. This does not limit cancellation or refund rights required by applicable law, including shipping delays. We will provide legally required delay notices and cancellation/refund options. This does not prevent leaving an unpaid Checkout Session before purchase and does not decide the still-pending ordinary return policy.
- We propose initiating approved refunds to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item is received and inspected, or after a refund without return is agreed. Your bank may take additional time to post the credit. Any legally required earlier refund deadline takes precedence. Refunds are processed manually through Stripe, not automatically by this website.
- No blanket “handmade,” artwork or final-sale exception is proposed. Any custom/personalized-product exception requires separate approval and prominent pre-purchase disclosure; it cannot remove applicable rights for defects or misdescription. This policy does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

## 3. Privacy / 隐私

**English — proposed notice; verify operational details before publication**

Our hosting/payment providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure their services.

Stripe hosts payment collection and processes payment details. AbilityMade's order system stores payment references and status, not full card numbers or security codes. Suppliers should receive only the information necessary for their part of the order. We also use Railway for website/order hosting and Resend for newsletter and supported email services. Our site uses browser local storage to retain your cart and support checkout retries. Clearing that storage may remove your cart or retry safeguards but does not cancel or refund a payment. Do not rely on a shared browser to protect your order references.

**Proposed commitment, subject to owner/vendor-practice verification:** we do not sell customer personal information or share it for third-party targeted advertising. Newsletter subscription is optional and can be cancelled using the unsubscribe option in marketing emails or by contacting us.

We restrict operational access and retain information for order service, accounting, dispute resolution and applicable legal requirements. The final retention schedule and deletion process must be confirmed before publication. Contact AbilityMade@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion; we may verify identity and retain information where required or permitted by law. Supplier and provider handling, international processing locations, any analytics/cookies, and applicable state-specific disclosures must be verified before this notice is finalized. Do not send card numbers, passwords or identification documents by ordinary email.

## Territory compatibility and cost risks — verified documentation, no API transaction

As of this review, Stripe’s official Checkout allowed-country list includes US, PR (Puerto Rico) and GU (Guam). AS (American Samoa), MP (Northern Mariana Islands), VI (US Virgin Islands) and UM (US Minor Outlying Islands) are absent and cannot be relied on as supported standalone shipping-country options. This is API-documentation verification, not a hosted address-entry test on our pinned API version. Do not mislabel an address as US to bypass a restriction. Whether any territory is accepted as a US state/address representation needs explicit Stripe confirmation and end-to-end validation; it is not established here.

Checkout now requests exactly US, PR and GU; recovery accepts those countries and does not require a separate state for PR/GU. AS, MP, VI and UM are excluded. Hosted Sandbox tests remain required: US (including AK/HI/DC), PR and GU, and attempts to select excluded countries or enter excluded territories under US. Local mocks and documented country options do not prove hosted address validation. A post-payment recovery rejection cannot prevent a charge; if an excluded destination can pass hosted Checkout, resolve it before launch. Keep Automatic Tax false; supplier costs/delivery estimates and legal/tax obligations remain unresolved.

Official source: https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create?query=shipping_address_collection

Alaska/Hawaii and territories may involve greater carrier costs, longer transit, remote-area surcharges, package-size restrictions and customs forms depending on origin/destination. Multi-supplier packages can multiply AbilityMade’s costs while customer shipping stays $10 or $0. Obtain supplier-specific quotes by product weight/dimensions and destination, dispatch/transit estimates, tracking and damage/claim rules. No surcharge may be added to the approved customer rate without a new owner decision. Supplier shipping-payment timing, evidence requirements and cost approvals remain to be agreed. Do not apply the proposed contiguous-US 6–14-day total estimate to Alaska/Hawaii/territories without evidence.
